## What Koach is about

Koach is Kauvery Hospital’s learning platform. It gives hospital teams one dependable system for creating, assigning, governing and measuring clinical and non-clinical learning. Several hospitals run on it, each on its own domain, against one shared platform.

For learners, success means knowing at a glance what is due, what is next and where they stand, and finishing training and attendance without confusion. For administrators, it means completing complex training-management tasks accurately and seeing status and ownership at a glance.

| Surface | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Learner workspace | Home, the learning journey, classroom sessions, e-learning and the learning hub: what is due, what is next, tests, attendance and certificates. |
| Trainer and HOD consoles | Sessions, nominations, approvals, attendance validation and learner results. |
| Hospital admin console | Users, programmes, learning content, assignments, compliance, certificates, communications and reports for one hospital. |
| Platform admin console | Manages hospitals across the platform. |

## Principles

1. **Predictable administration.** Complex administration feels predictable through consistent hierarchy, controls and feedback.
2. **Stay oriented.** People always know where they are across hospital, programme, area, topic, unit and learner.
3. **Show state.** Loading, empty, error, lifecycle, assignment, validation and success are always visible.
4. **Reuse before inventing.** Use an established pattern before adding a new interaction or visual treatment.
5. **Tell the truth.** Show only what the server allows, and make the consequences of destructive actions clear.
