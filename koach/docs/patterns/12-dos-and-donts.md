# 12 · Do's and don'ts

Quick rules that keep every screen consistent.

_Koach Brand Book v1.0 · generated from index.html_

| Topic | Do | Don't |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buttons | Use one clear primary action and secondary styles for the rest. | Show several competing primary actions in one area. |
| Colour | Use semantic tokens for meaning and states. | Use raw hex values, or colour alone to show status. |
| Typography | Use approved text styles and hierarchy. | Add one-off font sizes or weights without review. |
| Forms | Show labels, validation and helpful error messages. | Rely on placeholder text, or show errors without a fix. |
| Cards & surfaces | Follow the surface rule consistently. | Combine borders and shadows at random. |
| Spacing & layout | Use the spacing scale, align to the grid and keep one focal point per view. | Add one-off gaps, or crowd a view with competing focal points. |
| Icons | Use the icon set at 16, 20 or 24 px. | Mix icon styles or use icons without clear meaning. |
| Tables & data | Keep headers, sorting, status and row actions consistent. | Use inconsistent alignment or hide status. |
| Feedback | Confirm results and say how to recover from errors. | Show success in the brand colour, or an error with no fix. |
| Content | Use plain words from the word list and the writing patterns. | Use jargon, blame, exaggerated promises or unclear claims. |
