## What Cura42 is about

Cura42 builds AI agents for hospitals. The agents work alongside a hospital’s own teams and stay in touch with each patient before, during and after care, so the next appointment, procedure and referral stay with the hospital that treated them.

Hospitals meet patients later than they should and lose touch with them earlier than they want to. Cura42 closes the gaps between visits without asking more of the care team.

| Product | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Digital Front Door | Patients find a doctor, book on any channel and get reports and reminders where they already book. |
| Care Coordination AI Agents | Agents prepare patients before a visit, guide them on the day and check in once they are home. |
| Patient Longitudinal Record | Each patient’s history from the EMR, HMS and home in one record that every tool builds on. |

## Principles

1. **Paper first.** About 90% of any screen is neutral. Brand red is about 5%; accents live only in illustrations.
2. **One red thing.** Each view has one brand-red focal point: the primary action or one accent phrase.
3. **Air is the luxury.** Sections breathe. When unsure, add space, not decoration.
4. **Lift, don’t shout.** Surfaces are flat. Depth appears only on hover and on floating layers.
5. **Say it plainly.** Short sentences, concrete outcomes, no hype.
