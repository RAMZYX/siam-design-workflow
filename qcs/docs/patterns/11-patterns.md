# 11 · Patterns

Recurring combinations of components and the rules behind them.

_QCS Brand Book v1.0 · generated from index.html_

## List pages

- Order: title and count, then search and filters, then the table, then pagination.
- One primary action on the right of the title row, 48 px.
- Search on the left; filters beside it; keep applied filters visible.
- Bulk actions appear in a selection bar only when rows are selected.
- Status is a dot and a word in its own column. Professionals, Service users, PAL audit and Manage organisations all follow this.

## Forms in modals

- Forms live in the 520 px modal. QCS has no drawer or side panel.
- Labels above fields, always visible; 48 px fields.
- Mark optional fields, not required ones.
- Validate on blur; keep the person’s input when there is an error.
- Cancel on the left of the action in the footer; Escape closes unless saving.

## Multi-step flows

- The PAL audit flow is Start, nine questions, review, result.
- Show where the person is and what is done; keys 1 to 4, Enter and arrows work in the checklist.
- Back steps back one question without losing answers.
- End with the finding in one sentence, then the detail.

## Empty, loading & error

- Loading: name what is loading; keep the layout still.
- Unread values show “—”, never 0.
- Empty: say what is missing and give one action to start.
- No results: say what was searched and offer to clear filters.
- Error: say what went wrong in plain words and offer Try again.
- Disabled: say why in a tooltip on the control.

## Feedback

- Snackbar: confirm an action that just finished. It closes by itself.
- Callout: keep a message next to the content it is about until it is resolved.
- Modal: ask for a decision before something hard to undo.
- Show one message at a time.
- Use success tokens for success and danger tokens only for failed or spent.

## Tables & data views

- Plain header row, striped flat rows, status as a dot and a word.
- Row actions are links on the right: Manage, Review.
- Paginate and say how many rows exist: “1–10 of 48”.
- The table scrolls inside its frame; the page never scrolls sideways.
- Show “Previous manager: X” only when the manager is archived.

## Dashboard

- Named sections: Overview, Adoption snapshot, Pending actions.
- Each card has an icon circle and a title, then numbers split by hairlines.
- Sections stack below 1180 px; a card’s inside never changes.

## Full-screen audit

- Opens over the whole app with a top bar and Back on the left.
- A sticky 300 px sidebar holds files and actions; one column holds the report.
- Long reports group areas into accordions, with the group that needs action first.
- The result states the finding in one sentence before the detail.

## Allowances

- Spell out the count: “8 of 10 used this month”.
- Teal while healthy, amber when running low, red when spent.
- When spent, block the action and say why: “No care plan audits left this month. You have used all 10.”
- Offer the way forward: Request increase.

## Navigation

- Flat teal sidebar; the active item is a square block.
- The QCS menu shows only Dashboard, Professionals, Service users, PAL audit and Settings.
- Page actions sit in the page, never in the header.
- Back returns to the list the person came from; Back to QCS leaves the organisation.
