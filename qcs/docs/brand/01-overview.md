## CareBrain, the platform behind QCS

QCS lives inside CareBrain. This is CareBrain as it describes itself on carebrain.ai.

CareBrain is “intelligence for better care”: an AI thinking partner that helps carers and managers deliver safer, smarter care. It is an AI-powered app and business transformation tool for the whole care sector, including adult, children’s and specialist care services.

Its approach is people-powered AI. It brings people, processes and culture together with technology so teams can give personalised, efficient and compassionate care. It is built to support carers, never to replace them.

The app works alongside a provider’s existing systems with no integration, runs on a secure walled-garden platform that keeps data protected, and works in English and other languages, by voice and by text. Ongoing consultancy is part of the service: CareBrain presents itself as a partnership, not just a product.

## What QCS is about

QCS organisations are a white-labelled product inside the CareBrain admin panel. They are listed on their own home page and carry the QCS palette: a deep pine-teal for structure and action, amber for anything inactive, and warm neutrals. A flag the backend sets when the organisation is created decides which product an organisation is; it is never inferred.

A QCS organisation gets a trimmed feature set: the Dashboard, Professionals, Service users, PAL audit and a reduced Settings area. For QCS administrators, success means seeing every organisation, its package and its allowance at a glance. For organisation managers, it means keeping their team, service users and care plans in order and knowing what needs action today.

## Principles

1. **Hue changes, structure does not.** QCS redefines colour tokens only. Radius, spacing, shadow and type are the design system, not the brand.
2. **Teal is the brand, amber is inactive.** Teal marks anything actionable. Amber means archived, gated or running low. Red means failed or spent, never merely archived.
3. **Status always carries a word.** A dot plus a word, a pill plus a label. Colour supports the label and never replaces it.
4. **Unread is a dash, never a zero.** “0 of 5 used” is a claim about the allowance. When the backend has not answered, show “—”.
5. **One pattern per job.** Once a card, button height, status pill or striped table exists, extend it. Never invent a second look for the same job.
6. **Hierarchy before decoration.** Fix hierarchy in this order: spacing, type size and weight, alignment, surface, colour, then borders and shadow.
