# 03 · Logo

The QCS mark is the whole logo. Use the supplied file at its original proportions, with its corners rounded, every time.

## Clear space

Keep clear space of at least a quarter of the tile’s width on every side (proposed; not yet confirmed by QCS).

## Minimum size

| Height | Use |
| --- | --- |
| 32 px | minimum (proposed) |
| 52 px | sidebar |

## Approved backgrounds

| Background | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| White surface | #FFFFFF |
| Canvas | #F4F6F5 |
| Sidebar teal | #0F3D3C |

## Never

- Stretch, squash or crop it; scale it evenly
- Recolour the tile or the bubble, or cut the bubble out of its tile
- Show it with square corners; round them to about a fifth of the width
- Add shadows, outlines, glows or effects
- Place it on a photograph or a busy pattern
- Redraw it in code or type the letters QCS in its place
