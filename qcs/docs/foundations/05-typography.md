## Rules

**Do**

- Use Lato for everything; weight shows rank: 700 titles and numbers, 600 actions and names, 500 labels, 400 body.
- Keep the page title (26 px) the largest text, except the 30 px usage number.
- Use tabular figures for counts, percentages and dates.
- Wrap a long label inside its column before reducing the type size.

**Don't**

- Add a second typeface, or a size or weight outside the scale.
- Go below 12 px for anything a person reads (the 11.5 px sidebar box is the one exception).
- Change the Lato font link as a side effect: the app loads 300, 400 and 700 only, so 500 and 600 are synthesised (a known gap).
